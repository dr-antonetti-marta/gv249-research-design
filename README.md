# GV249 Research Design in Political Science: lecture slides

Lecture slides for **GV249 Research Design in Political Science**, an undergraduate course in the Department of Government at the London School of Economics, 2026–27. The course introduces students to designing and evaluating research in political science: description, causation, experiments and quasi-experimental designs.

The topics and structure follow the version of GV249 developed by **Florian Foos** (LSE). The slides are my own: redesigned and rewritten from scratch for 2026–27 by [Marta Antonetti](https://github.com/dr-antonetti-marta), course convener.

Course-specific logistics and assessment material (quizzes, exams, Moodle content) are not included.

**Course book:** Bueno de Mesquita, E. and Fowler, A. (2021). *Thinking Clearly with Data*. Princeton University Press.
